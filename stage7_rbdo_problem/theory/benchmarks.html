Two closed-form benchmarks¶

Two further problems have a closed-form solution and are used to validate the parameter-dependent construction end to end. They are implemented in python/test/t_RobustOptimizationProblemCanonicalizer_benchmarks_std.py and are also the two gallery examples plot_canonical_correlated_normal.py and plot_canonical_uniform_bound.py, so the figures and the test exercise the same construction.

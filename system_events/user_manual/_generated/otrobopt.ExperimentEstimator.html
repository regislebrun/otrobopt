ExperimentEstimator¶

class otrobopt.ExperimentEstimator(*args)¶

Sampling estimator of a system-event probability.

The experiment generates a sample and weights , normalized by their sum. The probability is the weighted mean of the Boolean predicate evaluated on the indicators of the limit-state components,

The error estimate is the binomial standard error at the effective sample size . It is exact for independent Monte Carlo sampling and conservative for low-discrepancy or Latin hypercube sampling, whose true variance is lower. It is NaN when the effective sample size does not exceed one.

The estimator has no geometry. Reproducibility is controlled by the global random generator seed.

Parameters:
experimentopenturns.WeightedExperiment

Experiment used to generate the sample. Its distribution is replaced by the distribution passed to the estimator at each call.

Examples

>>> import openturns as ot
>>> import otrobopt
>>> f = ot.ParametricFunction(
...     ot.SymbolicFunction(["x", "theta"], ["x - theta"]), [1], [0.0]
... )
>>> predicate = ot.SymbolicFunction(["y0"], ["y0"])
>>> estimator = otrobopt.ExperimentEstimator(ot.MonteCarloExperiment(10000))
>>> probability = estimator.getSafeProbability(predicate, f, ot.Normal(), [0.0])
>>> abs(probability - 0.5) < 0.02

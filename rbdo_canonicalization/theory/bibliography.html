Bibliographic notes and references¶

Bibliographic notes¶

The literature review of RBDO led to the following design choices. The double-loop formulation with RIA or PMA is the reference implementation [Aoues2010], [Tu1999], [Valdebenito2010]. SORA decouples the loops through a shift of the deterministic constraints [Du2004], and the single-loop methods replace the inner reliability problem by a fixed-point update [Liang2004]. The sample-average and scenario approximations are distribution-free references [Shapiro2009], [Calafiore2006], [Rocchetta2021], while joint chance constraints over finite distributions admit exact mixed-integer formulations [Luedtke2010], [Ahmed2018]. Analytic approximations and gradients for joint chance constraints are available for specific families [Geletu2019], [Xie2019], [VanAckooij2014], and sequential convex approaches require additional structure [Hong2011].

The classical robust optimization background is in [BenTal2009], [Beyer2007] and [Bertsimas2004]. Reliability-based design and its benchmarks are covered by [Aoues2010], [Valdebenito2010], [Du2004], [Tu1999], [Liang2004], [Lemaire2009], [Ditlevsen1996], [Hasofer1974] and [Rackwitz1978]. Chance constraints and stochastic programming are treated in [Prekopa1995], [Shapiro2003], [Shapiro2009], [Nemirovski2006], [Chen2010], [Rockafellar2000] and [Calafiore2006]. The isoprobabilistic constructions are due to [Rosenblatt1952], [Nataf1962], [Hohenbichler1981], [Lebrun2009a], [Lebrun2009b] and [Lebrun2009c]. Measure-theoretic and Monte Carlo foundations are in [Billingsley1995], [Bogachev2007] and [Glasserman2003]. The reparameterization identity used by stochastic optimization is discussed by [Kingma2014] and [Rezende2014].

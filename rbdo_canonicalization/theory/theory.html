Robust optimization theory¶

This page introduces the notation and the problem classes of robust optimization in otrobopt. The mathematical core is in Pushforward, invariance and canonicalization: the law-determined property, the pushforward identity, the invariance theorem, the parameter-dependent construction and the discretization. The measure catalogue is in Robust optimization measures, the specialized algorithms in Specialized algorithms, the validation benchmarks in Worked examples and validation, and the references in Bibliographic notes and references. The class reference is in the User manual.

The following assumptions are maintained throughout.
